## intent

lightweight script that is basically s live spreadsheet with a ui. in torn there are many educations and they take several years to complete all. rather than having spreadsheets all over the internet for everyone to do this solo over and over, create a simple script that plugs in all the variables (allows the user to change them) and the users own data from the education page to calculate the total time to complete a set of courses based on the prerequisites and modifiers and give the final date and time. should allow a user to enter any modifiers and how many (job points for 30 mins off, books of carol, etc) then enter into a text box any part of the education name and get suggestions in a dropdown, then they select and run. the output should walk them through each course they will need to complete in sections like 1. must do first, then 2. all of these but in any order, 3. last course (the one they picked) - or something clear like that. with dates and times for completion of each next to the courses in order for the courses. needs to be simple and run collecting browser data.


## some guide/forum examples from torn.com

    1.  Education
    Education is an important long-term systems in Torn City taking over 6 Years *without reduction perks to complete.

    You can only study one course at a time, and courses take real time days or weeks to complete. Once finished, their bonuses are permanent. Because of this, starting education early is critical. Any time your education slot is empty is lost progress.

    Education affects almost every part of the game, including training efficiency, crime success, fighting ability, and access to advanced content later on. Many of these bonuses are subtle, but they compound heavily over time.

    Early on, there are some borderline essential Educations to complete, I recommend the following Education Path;

    Introduction to Biology
    Intravenous Therapy - Unlocks Blood Bags (TORN's best Medical Item)
    Introduction to Sports Science
    The rest of Sports Science prioritising Learning Outcomes that Increase Gym Gains and Passive Stat bonuses first
    Bachelor of Sports Science - Permanent increase gym gains
    Back to Biology prioritising Courses with Learning Outcomes first
    Bachelor of Biology - Unlock Syringes (Temporarily able to buff your stats making fighting easier)
    Whatever you want! 

    This order of Education unlocks Blood Bags, Syringes, Permanent increased Gym Gains, Damage, Medical Item effectiveness and more! By the time this is achieved you will likely have more of an understanding about what you want to focus on in TORN, Know how to see what you are going to unlock in future and be able to make a more informed decision about what you want to go into next.

    Company Ownership? More Fighting Perks? Gym Gains Focused? Crime Progression? The choice is yours!

 2. Dummy’s Guide to Education

Education is a part of Torn where you can unlock certain areas of the game and earn passive gains. You can also gain a small amount of job stats with education.

I always recommend you are doing an education course. They are very cheap and they will take a while to complete, so the sooner you start the better. It will take years to finish all of the education courses.

Completing all of the education courses will cost you 225,785 dollars. This is incredibly cheap considering that it takes years to complete education. If it took you 6.75 years (without any upgrades) to complete all of the education courses you would only have to spend 92 dollars every day.

What is an introduction course?

An introduction course is a course that you must complete before starting all the other courses in that area. Introductions will always take 1 week to complete, and will always cost 200 dollars.

What is a bachelor course?

A bachelor course is the final course in an area. To be able to start this, you need to complete all the other education courses in that one area. A bachelor will always unlock an unique bonus or location. You will have to spend 5,000 dollars to start this, and it will take 5-7 weeks to complete, depending on which course you choose.

How do I reduce education time?
Spend merits to decrease education, giving up to a 20% decrease in education time.
Join a 1 star Fitness Center or a 7 star Hair Salon, where at that point you can unlock a job special, which will let you use one job point to subtract 30 minutes of time from your current education time. Joining a 10 star company of either type will let you get rid of 5 hours of education time every 24 hours, if you spend all your job points on that.
Getting to the rank of "Principal" in the Education starter job, will give you a passive bonus of -10% education time.
A Book of Christmas Carols will let you get you past 2 hours of education time, but right now buying this on the item market will cost you 14 million. You can also find this in Christmas Town, but you might as well sell it.
Purchasing 1 million shares of West Side South Bank University Block on the Stock Market will reduce your education time by 10%. To unlock the Stock Market you need to spend 20 points.

Appendix:

Q: How does Weapon Experience work?
A: The idea is that the more you use a specific weapon - say, a Minigun - the better you get at using that weapon. This Minigun experience does not apply to an Arma-Lite, a Macana, or a Jackhammer, etc. ... but it does apply to any Minigun. Then if you put down the Minigun and start using something else, you'll start gaining experience with that other weapon. And next time you pick up any Minigun, you'll retain your previous knowledge and skill.

Q: What about Education reduction perks?
A: Get as many as you can as soon as you can! By spending Merits (up to -20%), getting to Principal in the Education Job (-10%), buying the WSSB stock benefit block (-10%), using Job Points in either a Hair Salon or Fitness Center (.5h per point), or using the rare booster Book of Carols (-2h for 6h Booster CD). Your specific perks don't affect the order of the courses list above, though they do reduce the Course Length and Days per Bonus numbers. The -% perks don't stack additively, but are multiplied together. So the max you can get from perks is .8*.9*.9 = .648 = 35.2% time reduction before job points or boosters.

C. WAYS TO REDUCE EDUCATION TIME:


Merits: Your first merits should be in education, as you won't have enough money to maximize on bank merits anyway, to start. This provides a 20% bonus to education reduction at 10 merits. Every new to mid player should have these.

WSU BB/Benefit Block: 1 million shares of WSU will provide you another 10% bonus to education reduction. You must own the stock for 7 days before taking the new course. It will basically charge for 7 days before becoming active. This one is trickier since it costs around 100 million to own, but is a goal you should be working towards. 
Because the stock block is useless during the actual course, you might ask people in your faction if you can borrow their block during a longer course they have (particularly the bachelor courses). It's harder to time than the bank BB but worth a shot if your faction has good trust

Torn City Starter Job - Education: Tesla's City Career Fast-Tracking Guide provides more details, but becoming a Principal in the education system provides another 10% boost that you can achieve in the late early game. This is a goal you should be working towards.

Player Company Perks: Player owned Fitness Centers and Hair Salons both allow you to exchange 1 job point for a half hour of education reduction. A fitness center is the better company at 10 but also more difficult to be employed at due to work stat requirements. Either of these companies at 10 provide a 5 hour reduction every 24 hours which is basically a 20% bonus to reduction (there are better companies for new players than these but it's a method). 

Book of Carols: Book of Carols is an expensive booster item (13-14 million) that will reduce 6 hours of education time and adds a 6 hour booster cooldown. Some "dedicated newbies" might use this item to fast track education but the only time you should ever consider this item is when your education ends after you go to bed and you can save 6 hours plus whatever time you would have wasted while asleep by not entering a new course. It's debatable whether even a 7-12 hour reduction is worth it to a new player with limited cash.
